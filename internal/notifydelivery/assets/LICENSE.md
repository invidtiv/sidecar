# Sidecar notification sound assets

The `attention.wav`, `done.wav`, and `failure.wav` files in this directory were generated specifically for Sidecar from simple synthesized tones. Marcus Buffett and contributors dedicate these files to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The source is the deterministic generator in `gen/main.go`; no third-party recording or sample is included.
